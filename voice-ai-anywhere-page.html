Frequently Asked Questions

What does "Voice AI Anywhere" mean?

Voice AI Anywhere means you can deploy your conversational AI agent across multiple channels - shareable browser links, website widgets, and phone lines - all from a single platform. Your customers can interact with your AI wherever they prefer.

Do users need to download an app?

No. Bolka's voice AI works directly in web browsers using WebRTC technology. Users simply click a link or interact with your website widget - no downloads, no plugins, no friction.

Can I use multiple deployment options at once?

Yes! You can share voice links, embed widgets on your website, AND connect phone lines simultaneously. All conversations flow into the same dashboard with unified analytics.

How is this different from a chatbot?

Chatbots use text. Bolka uses voice. Voice AI provides 3x higher engagement, faster resolution times, and better accessibility for users who prefer speaking over typing. Learn more about voice vs. chatbots.

What browsers are supported?

Bolka works on all modern browsers including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge on both desktop and mobile devices.
